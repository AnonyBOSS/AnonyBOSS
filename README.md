### [Distributed GPU Load Balancer for LLMs](https://github.com/AnonyBOSS/llm-distributed-gpu-load-balancer)

Distributed AI serving platform handling **1000+ concurrent LLM requests** across GPU-backed workers — **4 load-balancing strategies**, a real **RAG pipeline**, and fault tolerance. Runs as **9 Dockerized FastAPI microservices** with **Prometheus + Grafana** observability, a pytest CI suite, fault-injection benchmarks, and real **Qwen2.5 GPU inference** with continuous batching.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Docker` `Prometheus` `Grafana` `Hugging Face`

### [LLM Customer Feedback Analyzer](https://github.com/AnonyBOSS/NVIDIA-DLI-PromptEngineering-Assessment)

Automated analysis pipeline that chains prompt-engineered steps with **LangChain** and **Llama-3.1-8B** (via NVIDIA AI Endpoints) to classify **sentiment**, pinpoint the **product categories and store locations** driving negative feedback, and generate an executive summary.

`LangChain` `Llama 3.1` `NVIDIA AI Endpoints` `Python`

### [UniManage — University Management System](https://github.com/AnonyBOSS/University-Management-System-MVP)
*Production-Ready MVP*

University platform with **role-based access** (student / professor / admin), course enrollment with **capacity enforcement**, assignment submission & grading, classroom booking with **non-overlapping time enforcement**, real-time messaging, and announcements.

`Next.js 16` `Supabase` `TypeScript` `Tailwind v4`

### [Clinify — Healthcare Platform (Mobile + Web)](https://github.com/AnonyBOSS/Clinify-mobile-app)

End-to-end healthcare product spanning a **React Native mobile app** and a **Next.js web app**: doctor search and appointment booking with **strict schedule validation** (no double-booking, room and maintenance constraints), an **AI health assistant & symptom checker**, real-time chat, ratings, role-specific dashboards, and Arabic/English support.

`React Native` `Expo` `Next.js` `TypeScript` `REST API`
